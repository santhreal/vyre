# Vyre architecture

The charter is the workspace [`README.md`](README.md). That file places
every crate and states the two rules: composed, not rewritten; intrinsic
means uncomposable.

[`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) is the short page the
architecture gate checks against the live tree. [`THESIS.md`](THESIS.md)
is the design argument. Per-crate `README` files apply the charter to one
crate.
